# Business Requirements

## Project Title

Enterprise Bank Fraud Analytics

## Business Problem

Banks process a large number of financial transactions every day. Identifying fraudulent activity from large transaction volumes requires systematic analysis of transaction characteristics, customer behavior, device information and risk indicators.

The objective of this project is to create an analytical solution that helps identify fraud patterns and provides actionable insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Objectives

The project aims to:

- Monitor total transaction volume
- Identify fraudulent transactions
- Calculate fraud rate
- Compare fraudulent and legitimate transactions
- Analyze fraud by payment type
- Analyze fraud by device operating system
- Analyze fraud by employment status
- Analyze fraud by housing status
- Analyze customer risk characteristics
- Analyze credit risk
- Analyze foreign versus domestic requests
- Analyze fraud patterns by age group
- Identify high-risk and very-high-risk transactions
- Provide interactive filtering and reporting

## Key Business Questions

### Transaction Analysis

- How many transactions were processed?
- How many transactions were fraudulent?
- How many transactions were legitimate?
- What is the overall fraud rate?
- How does fraud vary over time?

### Customer Analysis

- Which employment categories have higher fraud volumes?
- Which housing categories are associated with higher fraud?
- Which age groups show higher fraud activity?
- What is the average credit limit?

### Risk Analysis

- Which risk categories have the highest fraud volume?
- What is the average fraud risk score?
- What is the fraud rate for different credit risk categories?
- How does proposed credit limit relate to credit risk?

### Behavioral Analysis

- Which payment types have the highest fraud volume?
- Which device operating systems are associated with more fraud?
- How do payment types and device OS interact?
- What proportion of requests are foreign?
- What is the relationship between session behavior and fraud?

## Dashboard Requirements

The Power BI dashboard should provide:

- Interactive filters
- KPI cards
- Trend analysis
- Category comparisons
- Risk analysis
- Behavioral analysis
- Matrix/heatmap analysis
- Interactive visual exploration

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 — Executive Fraud Overview

Purpose:

Provide a high-level view of the bank's fraud situation.

### Page 2 — Customer and Risk Analysis

Purpose:

Analyze customer characteristics and risk indicators associated with fraudulent activity.

### Page 3 — Fraud Pattern and Behavioral Analysis

Purpose:

Identify detailed behavioral and transactional patterns associated with fraud.

## Expected Outcome

The final solution should provide a centralized analytical dashboard that allows users to understand fraud trends, identify high-risk segments and investigate important fraud patterns.
